To My Most Gracious Lady

Though I wished to say these words to Your Grace myself, I fear my courage has never quite matched the depth of my heart. Whenever I try to talk to you, the things I have rehearsed a hundred times quietly abandon me, leaving me with little more than admiration and silence. You snatch my words from me like you have snatched my heart. Thus, I have chosen to entrust these thoughts to this humble parchment, hoping it may speak where I cannot. I ask only that Your Grace receive these words with the same kindness with which they were written. They carry no grand promises, no clever poetry, and no hidden purpose beyond the simple wish to tell you what my heart has long struggled to say. Every line that follows is sincere, and every word has been written with you in mind. If, after granting this poor letter a moment of your time, Your Grace still finds me worthy of your attention, then I humbly beseech you to continue reading what follows.
