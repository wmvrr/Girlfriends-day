To My Most Gracious Lady

**My Dearest,** It is a curious thing that two souls, unknown to one another and separated by distance, should cross paths through nothing more than a few words upon a screen. Had someone told me then that such a meeting would one day become so precious to me, I would scarcely have believed it. Yet, that is exactly what came to pass. What began as simple conversations slowly became the brightest part of my days. I found myself waiting for your messages with a patience I never possessed before, smiling at my phone for reasons only you could understand, and discovering that even an ordinary day seemed lighter whenever it contained a little piece of you. I have often wondered how someone I had never met could come to mean so much to me. I still do not know the answer. Perhaps hearts have a way of recognizing one another long before minds can explain why. There is nothing extraordinary about me, nor have I ever been particularly gifted with beautiful words. But if there is one truth I know without hesitation, it is that meeting you changed something within me. You made me laugh more easily, hope more freely, and believe that even the most unexpected encounters can become life's greatest blessings. Every conversation with you has become a memory I quietly treasure. Every small detail you have shared, every laugh, every teasing remark, every moment of care—you may have forgotten many of them, but I have not. They have become part of the story I cherish most. If there is one thing I would ask of fate, it is simply this: let me continue making memories with you. Let today's conversations become tomorrow's stories, and let tomorrow's stories become a lifetime of moments we can look back upon together. For in all the countless ways this vast world could have brought two strangers together, I am endlessly grateful that it chose to let me find you. **Yours, always.**

Mwah
